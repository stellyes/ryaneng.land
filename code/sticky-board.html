What is this?

A shared corkboard for anyone who wanders by. Pin up a typed note, a drawing, or a photo, and put it wherever you like.

  • One note per device, per month. Choose wisely.
  • The board is wiped clean at midnight (Pacific) on the 1st of every month.
  • Newest notes land on top. If someone covers yours, press Display note to see its outline and contents above everything else.
  • Once a day you can bump a covered note back to the top.
  • Click your own note (or press Move note) to drag and rotate it.
